Please note that you may apply for a short-term Business Visa through the Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) system.

However, approval of the Business Visa is subject to the submission of the following documents to the Department of Immigration and Emigration, Battaramulla, through the Sri Lankan sponsor/guarantor for verification and clearance purposes.

To avoid delays in visa processing, applicants are kindly advised to promptly submit the following documents:

  • Request letter from the sponsor/guarantor indicating the purpose of visit, duration of stay, and accommodation details of the applicant in Sri Lanka
  • Certified copy of the sponsor/guarantor’s Passport or National Identity Card (NIC)
  • Business Registration Certificate of the sponsor company
  • Copy of the applicant’s Passport
  • Applicant’s return air ticket

Your cooperation and assistance in fulfilling the visa processing requirements are highly appreciated.
